About me

Alireza Shirazi

I am an M.Sc. student in Smart Building Engineering at Tarbiat Modares University, Tehran. My research interests include concrete durability, low-carbon concrete and nanotechnology in construction materials.

I hold a B.Sc. in Civil Engineering from the University of Guilan. Alongside my graduate studies, I teach high school mathematics and have worked as a teaching assistant in Structural Dynamics.

My current thesis examines the effect of silica nanoparticles on self-healing concrete incorporating lightweight expanded clay.
